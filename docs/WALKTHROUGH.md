# A five-minute tour

Use the sample password from the [README](../README.md) for each account.

1. Sign in as `cashier`. Search for `skyblue shirtt 24` to try the typo-tolerant search. Add a product, finish a Cash sale and open the receipt.
2. Open **Transaction History and Refunds**. Refund part of a sample sale. The refund uses the original sale price and cannot exceed the quantity left to refund.
3. Sign out, then sign in as `south-cashier`. Open **Stock Requests** and receive the sample hub delivery. The second shop's stock increases once.
4. Sign in as `manager`. Open **Hubs** and **Workshop**. Look at Fictional Maker A's jobs and advance, or add a small tailoring job for a garment. The app updates stock and earnings together.
5. Sign in as `owner`. Browse the reports and switch shops. This account can view records but cannot change them.

Use made-up information throughout. Payment entries do not send money. Backups and cloud connections are disabled. Changes to the sample records can be cleared with the reset command in the README.
