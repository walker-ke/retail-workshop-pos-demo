# Five-minute walkthrough

1. Sign in as `cashier` with the sample password in the README. Search `skyblue shirtt 24` to see spacing/typo handling. Add a product and make a fictional Cash sale. View its printable receipt.
2. Open Transaction History and Refunds. Refund part of a sample sale; the remaining quantity and original sold price govern the refund.
3. Sign out and use `south-cashier`. Open Stock Requests and receive the seeded fictional hub delivery. It changes the second shop's stock once.
4. Use `manager` for Hubs and Workshop. Inspect Fictional Maker A's production and advance, or record a small tailoring job against a garment variant. Earnings and hub stock update together.
5. Use `owner` to browse dashboards/reports and switch shops. This account is read-only: mutation requests are rejected even if submitted manually.

There are no real payment links, bank details, customer identities or cloud accounts. The app's Backups section is unavailable in demo mode. Do not enter real information; all writes are disposable.
